General links

https://remote.le.ac.uk/ — most of the useful links all in one place, some are listed below:

  1. https://blackboard.le.ac.uk/ultra/course — I find the website works better than the app
  2. https://outlook.office.com/mail/ — download the app on your phone and I highly recommend adding the calendar widget to your home screen
  3. https://mystudentrecord.le.ac.uk/ — mitigating circumstances, exam results, documents, OLMS
  4. https://uniofleicester.sharepoint.com/sites/student — MyWorkspace sharepoint website: interesting news and events
  5. https://le.ac.uk/library — search the library database, both physical and online books
  6. https://connect2.le.ac.uk/ — David Wilson Library group study room bookings
  7. https://myuol.le.ac.uk/dashboard/home — MyUoL app website (useful to check attendance)
  8. Leicester Students' Union
    • https://www.leicesterunion.com/ — union news, student elections, Advice Service
    • https://www.leicesterunion.com/support/ — useful signposting
    • https://www.leicesterunion.com/opportunities/societies/findasociety/ — list of every single society and sports club
    • https://www.leicesterunion.com/opportunities/sports/findaclub/ — list of just the sports clubs
  9. opentimetable.le.ac.uk — timetables for all rooms, modules and courses across the university
